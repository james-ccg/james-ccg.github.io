who.txt

Pulled out of a convenience store run into another world. No cheat power, no stats screen, no sword. What he gets is Return by Death: when he dies, time rewinds to a checkpoint he does not choose, and he is the only one who remembers.

He cannot tell anyone - trying makes a hand close around his heart. So he carries every failure alone.

why_him.txt

Most isekai leads arrive competent. Subaru is the opposite: loud, self-pitying, sure that suffering earns him something. The story agrees with none of it.

He is not likeable because he is good at this. He is likeable because he is bad at it and keeps going — and the show says plainly that he is being a coward, then lets him hear it.
