Steam profile backgrounds

Find a background on Steam,
crop it here.

I don't run my own scraped copy of Steam's background catalog - Steam already has one, and it's the real, up-to-date source. This page links straight to it, then hands off to the Cropper to slice whatever you land on into Featured, Artwork, and Workshop Showcase pieces.

How it works

Three steps from "I like this background" to a ready-to-upload showcase set.

1

Find a background

Browse the Points Shop or Market below, or open a background you already own from your inventory.

2

Save the image

Right-click the full-size preview on Steam → Save image as. (Steam's background CDN blocks loading it by link directly into a browser tool - this is the one extra click that works around it.)

3

Upload it to the Cropper

Open the Cropper, select the saved image, and download your sliced, Steam-ready showcase set.

Got a direct image link instead (Imgur, Discord, etc.)? The Cropper also has a "paste a URL" box that skips the save-and-upload step entirely - it works for most image hosts, just not Steam's own background CDN, which doesn't allow other sites to load its images directly (a restriction on Steam's end, not something a browser-only tool can route around).
